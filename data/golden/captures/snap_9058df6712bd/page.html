Under the Freedom of Information Law (FOIL), you can get copies of New York City agency records, information, and other material.

You can also search the NYC Government Publications Portal for reports and materials produced by agencies.

Visit the NYC Government Publications Portal.

You can request a copy of a service request you submitted or another City agency record.

Agency Not Listed Below

If you don’t see the agency listed below, choose one of these options:

  • Submit your request on Open Records. To submit a FOIL request, you must log into or create a NYC.ID account.
  • Send a written request to the agency. You can get the address on the agency's website. Visit the NYC.gov Agency Directory for the full list of City agencies.

You can request a copy of a service request you submitted or another record from the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP).

Include in your request:

  • Description of the specific record you want
  • Service request number if applicable
  • Name
  • Address
  • Telephone number or email address

Online

Submit your request on Open Records.

By Fax

Fax your request to (718) 595-6543.

By Mail

FOIL Records Access Officer
Department of Environmental Protection
59-17 Junction Boulevard, 19th Floor
Flushing, NY 11373

What Happens Next

DEP will send you a confirmation when they receive your request. They will search for relevant records and contact you with results.

DEP will charge you 25 cents a page for copies of FOIL documents. Don't send any money until requested.

You can request a copy of a service request you submitted or another record from the Department of Transportation (DOT) such as:

  • Change in street sign regulation
  • Installation of a new street sign or traffic signal
  • Permits issued to contractors
  • Repair or reports of broken parking meters
  • Traffic or parking regulations on a certain street
  • Traffic incident causing a delay

Learn more about DOT FOIL requests.
