Building Facade Inspection

  • No Penalty Inspection Programs to learn about DOB's free No Penalty Safety Inspection Program for facades

Building six stories or higher must have their exterior walls and appurtenances (such as balconies) inspected every five years. They must be inspected by a New York State licensed architect or engineer.

Façade report and maintenance/repair deadlines are divided into cycles. Cycle 9 began February 21, 2020. It runs through February 20, 2025.

You must submit façade compliance filings online in DOB NOW: Safety.

Learn about building façade inspections.

File a job through DOB NOW.

You can learn more by phone.

Call 311 or 212-NEW-YORK (212-639-9675) for help.

The following must report hazardous defects on a building's exterior walls:

  • Licensed architects
  • Engineers

These must be reported right away. They can do so by submitting a Notification of UNSAFE Facade, FISP-3, in DOB NOW: Safety.

The building owner should begin to repair or stabilize the exterior walls right away.

The notice is not a violation or a ticket.

An inspector will:

  • Verify the hazardous conditions
  • Determine if enough safeguards have been provided
  • Determine if repairs are in progress

If no safeguards are in place, the inspector can issue a DOB violation.
