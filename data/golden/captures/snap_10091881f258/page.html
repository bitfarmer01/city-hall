Noise from Neighbor

Call 911
Call 911 to report fighting, screaming, gunshots, explosions, noise from a large party or crowd that is causing danger, or suspicious breaking of glass or wood.

Need something else?

  • Mediation for confidential, free, professional help from a trained mediator for noise issues
  • Noise Complaints to report other types of noise 
  • Noise Code

You can make a complaint about noise from your neighbor including loud music, party, or television, talking, and moving or dragging of furniture.

Report a noisy neighbor.

What Happens Next

Officers from the New York Police Department (NYPD) will respond within 8 hours when they are not handling emergencies. They will be able to take action if the noise is still happening when they arrive. 

Other Ways to Get Help

Many buildings have rules about noise. Contact your tenant association, building owner, or super if a neighbor in your building is often noisy.

NYC Noise is New York City’s Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) free iPhone and Android mobile app. The app will collect noise data for DEP to better understand noise levels, sources, and patterns.

The app does not submit noise complaint services requests.

NYC Noise is for data collection only. It provides DEP with:

  • Noise decibel level
  • Date, time, and source of noise
  • Defines noise by types:
    • Alarm
    • Car and bus traffic
    • Construction
    • Dog/animal
    • HVAC/fan
    • Loud music
    • Siren
    • Vehicle horn

Submitted data appears in map and table formats. Data will help DEP identify noise hotspots and guide their targeted enforcement efforts.

Learn more about DEP's NYC Noise App.
