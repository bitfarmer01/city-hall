Elevator or Escalator Inspection

Sign In | Sign Up
  Related links
NYC311 Home Report Problems Look Up Service Requests Browse Topics
Call 911
Call 911 to report an accident or a dangerous situation.

Need something else?

  • Elevator or Escalator Complaint
  • NYCHA Public Housing Maintenance Complaint to report an elevator or escalator problem in a NYCHA building
  • No Penalty Inspection Programs to learn about DOB's free No Penalty Safety Inspection Program for private elevators, dumbwaiters, platform lift, and stairway chair lifts

If you are a building owner, you must:

  • Get your elevator inspected each year
  • Hire a private agency to do the inspection
  • Submit the required files to the Department of Buildings (DOB)

Visit the DOB NOW: Safety page.

On the DOB NOW: Safety page, you can:

  • Register for DOB NOW: Safety
  • Submit files
  • Learn more

DOB also contracts with private companies to do regular inspections. These cannot be scheduled. 

If required, a licensed professional can schedule an inspection:

  • For new construction
  • For alteration elevator permits
  • To address a violation online in DOB NOW: Inspections

Visit the DOB NOW: Inspections page.

On the DOB NOW: Inspections page you can:

  • Register for DOB NOW: Inspections
  • Request an inspection appointment
  • Learn more
